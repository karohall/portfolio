ASS2ROID

A Borderland camp concept complete with cinematic trailer, scrappy asteroid rover, an ad campaign, and a guidebook. Welcome to Korova Rock. We go deep.

Role Concept Development, Art Direction, Storytelling
Tools Midjourney, ElevenLabs, Adobe CC, Canva
Venue The Borderland Festival
Year 2026

Concept

Two prospectors. One asteroid. Total commitment.

ASS2ROID is a theme camp for The Borderland — a co-created festival. The premise: two prospectors are running a full mining operation on the fictional asteroid Korova Rock. Everything that follows is built from that premise outward, with total sincerity and absolutely no restraint.

The project began as a collaborative creative exercise combining a shared love of sci-fi franchises and 1980s comics. The goal was to build a complete fictional universe — not a mood board, but a world with lore, visual language, physical objects, and a soundtrack.

Visual Direction

Pulpy, hand-made, resolutely not polished

The visual language draws from 1980s comic book artists — Richard Corben and Moebius in particular. The deliberate choice was to use AI tools (Midjourney, ElevenLabs) to achieve a hand-crafted, pulpy aesthetic rather than the smooth, clean output AI usually produces. Every frame of the trailer, every poster element, every sticker was pushed toward roughness.

The fictional publisher "Kroeber Institute" lends institutional legitimacy to the lore. The Latin mission motto — in profundum imis ("into the deepest depths") — is stitched onto the mission emblem with full seriousness.

Deliverables

The full universe

  • Cinematic trailer — comic book style, AI-generated visuals and audio
  • Movie poster
  • Dual ad campaigns referencing 1980s–90s visual culture
  • Official Korova Rock guidebook, credited to the Kroeber Institute
  • Menu concept (including references to "Milk+")
  • Sticker collection with original one-liners
  • Mission emblem with Latin motto
  • Comic strip
  • Physical rover bar — retrofitted garden cart with game consoles, tubing, speaker, and custom packaging
  • Curated 1980s soundtrack

The Physical Build

A rover bar in the dust

The physical installation is a retrofitted garden cart transformed into an asteroid mining rover. Game consoles, custom tubing, a speaker, and hand-made packaging complete the illusion. At a festival built on collective world-making, it's the object that makes the fiction feel real.
