01 Body of Work Exhibition Design & Artistic Platform
+
Exhibition Design & Artistic Platform

Haptics, sensors, and other new technologies are enabling art where your body becomes the material for the work. When touch becomes the site of the artwork, it can't be photographed or recorded. It exists only in your body. Touch is complex to work with. There is little terminology, few shared references, and no defined frameworks.

This event was part workshop, part exhibition, part laboratory, bringing together artists in Sweden who are defining this field to discuss its challenges and contradictions. And most importantly, to get a feel for what it is.

Co-initiated with Jakob Skote. Helix Art Space, June 2026.

Background

I move at the threshold between physical presence and digital interaction.

My earliest memories of digital space are reading cyberpunk classics and watching sci-fi, Blade Runner was a big one. As I grew older I was obsessed with games like Portal, exploring spatiality and emergent narratives within virtual worlds. Now I design those systems in addition to inhabiting them.

With roots in dance and performance, I bring spatial awareness and embodied cognition into digital design. My work spans UX design, XR installation, generative art, and brand systems in collaboration with artists, engineers, museums, and startups. I'm always asking how the container respects the human body moving through it, curious how space shapes movement and vice versa.

The best work happens in the spaces between.
