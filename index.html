Background

I think of interfaces as choreography — every scroll, tap, and transition is a gesture with intention

My path into digital design began through contemporary dance and physical theatre. After training at the Oslo National Academy of the Arts, I became fascinated with how bodies create meaning through movement — and how that logic could translate into digital space.

My work spans interactive installations, UX design, generative art, and motion systems — always returning to the question of how movement communicates. I've exhibited at Henie Onstad Kunstsenter, KODE Bergen, and Ars Electronica.

Currently, I split my time between client projects and artistic practice — developing tools and frameworks that bring choreographic thinking into everyday interface design.

Good motion design doesn't call attention to itself. It makes everything else feel inevitable.
