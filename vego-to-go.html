Brand Design — 2026

Vego to Go

Green fast food that feels relevant and fresh — a cross-touchpoint brand system exploring how digital clarity and physical play shape everyday experience for urban families.

Role Brand Strategy, Storytelling, Creative Direction
Tools Illustrator, Photoshop, WordPress, HTML/CSS
Year 2026

Overview

A brand system for healthy fast food

Vego to Go is a healthy fast-food concept launching mushroom tortellini to busy urban families. Three values shaped the entire strategic direction: simplicity, playfulness, and minimalism. The challenge was translating those values coherently across touchpoints that serve completely different emotional states.

The Challenge

Balancing clarity and play across three contexts

The same brand needed to work in three very different situations — each with different users, different emotional registers, and different interaction modes:

  • Website: A calm, clear, trust-building interface for research-minded parents making considered decisions
  • Packaging: Playful, tactile, engaging design for family supermarket moments — with children present
  • Product communication: Health, sustainability, and accessibility for decision-fatigued shoppers

Strategic Foundation

Nature as shared experience

The founding story became the strategic baseline. The brand was built around a concept the founders lived: urban families reconnecting with nature. Forest hikes during the pandemic. Healthy food as an accessible everyday ritual. Sustainability as a lifestyle expression, not a marketing claim.

This gave the brand something real to stand on — a reason for the values that didn't feel imposed or performative.

Design a cohesive brand experience across contexts with different users and expectations.

Digital Experience

  • Minimal, calm visual language
  • Clear information hierarchy
  • Transparency and fast decision-making support
  • Built on WordPress with custom HTML/CSS

Physical Experience

  • Playful visual language
  • Tactile interaction encouraged
  • Family-friendly tone and shared discovery
  • Consistent values, different expression

Approach

System thinking across every surface

The design logic is rooted in systems thinking: the same values (simplicity, playfulness, minimalism) are expressed differently depending on the emotional state of the user at each point. A parent researching online needs calm and clarity. A family in a supermarket with a five-year-old needs energy and delight. The brand accommodates both without contradiction.

Consistency isn't visual sameness. It's the same underlying logic, adapted to context.
